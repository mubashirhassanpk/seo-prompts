## SEO Basic Prompts
1. Keyword Research Prompts
Original Prompt: “Act like an SEO specialist. Give me a list of long-tail keywords related to [Main Keyword] that have informational search intent. Also, include potential variations for different user demographics.”
Additional Prompt: “Provide a list of 10 long-tail keywords for the topic '[Main Topic]' and explain their relevance to user intent. Include seasonal variations.”
2. Competitive Keyword Analysis
Original Prompt: “Analyze [competitor’s domain] and list the top-performing keywords they rank for, along with their estimated search volume and difficulty.”
Additional Prompt: “Identify the strengths and weaknesses in the keyword strategies of [Competitor A] and [Competitor B]. Provide insights into their content focus.”
3. On-page SEO Prompts
Original Prompt: “Generate 5 title tags and meta description variations for a blog post on ‘[Insert Topic]’. Keep the title tag under 60 characters and the meta description under 150 characters.”
Additional Prompt: “Create 3 engaging title tags and meta descriptions for the topic '[Insert Topic]'. Ensure they include a call to action and relevant keywords.”
4. Content Writing Prompts
Original Prompt: “Create a detailed blog outline for the topic and keyword ‘TOPIC’. Include H1, H2, and H3 tags.”
Additional Prompt: “Draft a comprehensive blog outline for ‘[Insert Topic]’, incorporating a unique angle or perspective that addresses user pain points.”
5. Internal Linking Suggestions
Original Prompt: “Given this list of existing blog topics and URLs, suggest 10 relevant internal links I can add to my new blog post titled ‘[Blog Post Title].’”
Additional Prompt: “Analyze the content of my latest blog post on ‘[Blog Post Title]’ and recommend internal links to at least 5 other posts on my site that enhance the user journey.”
6. Technical SEO Prompts
Original Prompt: “Act as a technical SEO auditor. Based on the following website URL ‘[Insert URL]’, list potential technical SEO issues to check for.”
Additional Prompt: “Evaluate the website ‘[Insert URL]’ for mobile optimization issues and suggest actionable improvements to enhance user experience.”
7. SEO Strategy and Analysis Prompts
Original Prompt: “Act like an SEO strategist. Analyze the following competitors: [Insert competitor domains].”
Additional Prompt: “Develop a competitive analysis report comparing SEO strategies of [Competitor A], [Competitor B], and [Competitor C]. Highlight opportunities for differentiation.”
8. SEO Audit Checklist
Original Prompt: “Act as an SEO auditor. Create a detailed SEO audit checklist for the website ‘[Insert URL]’.”
Additional Prompt: “Generate a comprehensive checklist for an SEO audit, emphasizing on-page, off-page, and technical aspects for websites in the ‘[Insert Industry]’ sector.”
9. Creating SEO Roadmaps
Original Prompt: “Create a 6-month SEO roadmap for a website in the ‘[Insert Industry]’ niche.”
Additional Prompt: “Outline a quarterly SEO strategy plan that includes new content creation, link-building initiatives, and performance metrics for a site in the ‘[Insert Niche]’ market.”
10. Data Analysis for SEO Reports
Original Prompt: “Here’s my Google Search Console export data: [Upload PDF data or CSV file as an attachment].”
Additional Prompt: “Analyze the past year of traffic data for my website [Insert URL] and identify trends, seasonal fluctuations, and areas needing content improvement.”
