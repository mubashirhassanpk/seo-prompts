## 1. SEO Basic Prompts
1. Keyword Research Prompts
Original Prompt: “Act like an SEO specialist. Give me a list of long-tail keywords related to [Main Keyword] that have informational search intent. Also, include potential variations for different user demographics.”
Additional Prompt: “Provide a list of 10 long-tail keywords for the topic '[Main Topic]' and explain their relevance to user intent. Include seasonal variations.”
2. Competitive Keyword Analysis
Original Prompt: “Analyze [competitor’s domain] and list the top-performing keywords they rank for, along with their estimated search volume and difficulty.”
Additional Prompt: “Identify the strengths and weaknesses in the keyword strategies of [Competitor A] and [Competitor B]. Provide insights into their content focus.”
3. On-page SEO Prompts
Original Prompt: “Generate 5 title tags and meta description variations for a blog post on ‘[Insert Topic]’. Keep the title tag under 60 characters and the meta description under 150 characters.”
Additional Prompt: “Create 3 engaging title tags and meta descriptions for the topic '[Insert Topic]'. Ensure they include a call to action and relevant keywords.”
4. Content Writing Prompts
Original Prompt: “Create a detailed blog outline for the topic and keyword ‘TOPIC’. Include H1, H2, and H3 tags.”
Additional Prompt: “Draft a comprehensive blog outline for ‘[Insert Topic]’, incorporating a unique angle or perspective that addresses user pain points.”
5. Internal Linking Suggestions
Original Prompt: “Given this list of existing blog topics and URLs, suggest 10 relevant internal links I can add to my new blog post titled ‘[Blog Post Title].’”
Additional Prompt: “Analyze the content of my latest blog post on ‘[Blog Post Title]’ and recommend internal links to at least 5 other posts on my site that enhance the user journey.”
6. Technical SEO Prompts
Original Prompt: “Act as a technical SEO auditor. Based on the following website URL ‘[Insert URL]’, list potential technical SEO issues to check for.”
Additional Prompt: “Evaluate the website ‘[Insert URL]’ for mobile optimization issues and suggest actionable improvements to enhance user experience.”
7. SEO Strategy and Analysis Prompts
Original Prompt: “Act like an SEO strategist. Analyze the following competitors: [Insert competitor domains].”
Additional Prompt: “Develop a competitive analysis report comparing SEO strategies of [Competitor A], [Competitor B], and [Competitor C]. Highlight opportunities for differentiation.”
8. SEO Audit Checklist
Original Prompt: “Act as an SEO auditor. Create a detailed SEO audit checklist for the website ‘[Insert URL]’.”
Additional Prompt: “Generate a comprehensive checklist for an SEO audit, emphasizing on-page, off-page, and technical aspects for websites in the ‘[Insert Industry]’ sector.”
9. Creating SEO Roadmaps
Original Prompt: “Create a 6-month SEO roadmap for a website in the ‘[Insert Industry]’ niche.”
Additional Prompt: “Outline a quarterly SEO strategy plan that includes new content creation, link-building initiatives, and performance metrics for a site in the ‘[Insert Niche]’ market.”
10. Data Analysis for SEO Reports
Original Prompt: “Here’s my Google Search Console export data: [Upload PDF data or CSV file as an attachment].”
Additional Prompt: “Analyze the past year of traffic data for my website [Insert URL] and identify trends, seasonal fluctuations, and areas needing content improvement.”

## 2. Advanced SEO Prompts
Keyword Research and Strategy
Long-Tail Keyword Research
"Act as an SEO specialist. For the keyword '[Primary Keyword]', generate 10 long-tail variations with high search volume and low competition. Classify each keyword by search intent (Informational, Navigational, Transactional, Commercial). Suggest content types (e.g., blog posts, videos, FAQs) for each keyword."
Competitor Keyword Analysis
"Analyze the domain '[Competitor URL]' and list their top-performing keywords. Include search volume, keyword difficulty, and ranking position. Suggest 5 keywords they rank for that I can target to outperform them."
Seasonal Keyword Trends
"Using Google Trends, identify seasonal trends for the keyword '[Insert Keyword]' over the past 12 months. Suggest content ideas to target peak search periods and maintain relevance during off-peak times."
Keyword Clustering
"Cluster the following keywords based on semantic relationships and user intent: [Insert Keyword List]. For each cluster, suggest a pillar page topic and supporting blog topics."
On-Page SEO
Title Tags and Meta Descriptions
"Generate 5 variations of title tags and meta descriptions for the blog post '[Insert Topic]'. Ensure the title is under 60 characters and the meta description is under 150 characters. Include the primary keyword '[Keyword]' and a call-to-action."
Heading Structure Optimization
"Review the following blog outline: [Insert Outline]. Suggest an SEO-friendly heading structure (H1, H2, H3) with relevant keyword placements. Ensure the structure aligns with search intent."
Internal Linking Suggestions
"Given this list of existing blog topics and URLs, suggest 10 relevant internal links for my new blog post titled '[Blog Post Title]'. Prioritize anchor text diversity and relevancy."
Content Refresh Strategy
"Analyze my blog '[Insert Blog URL]' and identify outdated content. Suggest updates to align with current search intent, add new keywords, and improve readability. Provide a step-by-step plan for refreshing the content."
Technical SEO
Technical SEO Audit
"Act as a technical SEO auditor. Analyze the website '[Insert URL]' for crawl errors, broken links, slow page speed, duplicate content, missing meta tags, and sitemap issues. Provide actionable steps to fix each issue."
Schema Markup Generation
"Generate a valid JSON-LD schema markup for a blog post titled '[Insert Title]'. Include fields like headline, author, datePublished, articleBody, and relevant keywords."
Core Web Vitals Optimization
"Analyze the Core Web Vitals performance for my website '[Insert URL]'. Suggest advanced fixes for improving LCP (Largest Contentful Paint), FID (First Input Delay), and CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift)."
Backlink Analysis
"Analyze the backlink profile of '[Insert URL]' using the following data: [Insert Backlink Data]. Identify toxic links, high-authority links, and opportunities for new backlinks. Summarize the profile’s strengths and weaknesses."
SEO Strategy and Reporting
Competitor Analysis
"Act as an SEO strategist. Analyze the following competitors: [Insert Competitor Domains]. Summarize their top-ranking keywords, content gaps, and backlink strategies. Provide recommendations to outperform them."
SEO Roadmap
"Create a 6-month SEO roadmap for a website in the '[Insert Industry]' niche. Break down tasks by month, covering keyword strategy, technical improvements, content creation, link building, and performance tracking. Include measurable KPIs for each stage."
CTR Optimization
"Here is the past 90 days of GSC data for my site: [Attach Data]. Identify keywords with a CTR below 2% but ranking in the top 10. Recommend meta title and description rewrites to improve CTR."
2. Advanced E-commerce Prompts
Store Setup and Optimization
Dropshipping Business Setup
"Create a step-by-step to-do list for starting a dropshipping business using Shopify. Include tasks like product research, store setup, payment gateway configuration, and marketing strategies. Use ZikAnalytics to identify 5 high-demand products with at least a 75% profit margin and match them with reliable suppliers from AliExpress."
Product Page Optimization
"For the following products [Insert Product List], create SEO-optimized product descriptions that include primary and long-tail keywords. Suggest additional elements like FAQs, customer reviews, and cross-selling opportunities to improve conversions."
Email Marketing Automation
"Integrate my Shopify store with Klaviyo and create a complete email sequence for abandoned cart recovery. Include a discount code to encourage conversions. Additionally, set up an automated funnel for post-purchase engagement, including order confirmation emails, tracking updates, and a follow-up email offering a 25% discount for customers who share their purchase on social media."
Social Media and Advertising
Social Media Content Calendar
"Using the raw videos and images provided, create a 30-day social media content calendar for promoting my dropshipping store. Include platform-specific strategies for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook. For each post, suggest captions, hashtags, and call-to-actions."
Facebook Ads Optimization
"Using the raw materials provided, create engaging video and image ads for Facebook. Set up targeting based on audience demographics and interests. Optimize the ads daily to maintain a ROAS of at least 3.5. Provide a daily activity report summarizing performance."
3. Advanced Automation Prompts
Workflow Automation
"Design an automated workflow for managing my e-commerce store. Include tasks like inventory management, order fulfillment, email marketing, and customer support. Suggest tools and integrations for Shopify, Klaviyo, and Zapier to streamline operations."
Blogging Automation
"I want to automate my blogging process using AI. Suggest a workflow for generating blog topics, creating outlines, writing drafts, and optimizing for SEO. Include tools like ChatGPT for content creation, SurferSEO for optimization, and Grammarly for editing."
Data Analysis Automation
"Set up an automated workflow for analyzing my website's Google Analytics and Search Console data. Include tasks like generating weekly reports, identifying trends, and sending alerts for significant changes in traffic or rankings."
4. Advanced Niche Research Prompts
Micro-Niche Blogging Strategy
"I want to start a blog targeting non-English-speaking audiences. Suggest 3 macro niches specifically targeting men or women with informational content. Under each macro niche, provide 3 micro niches with high search volume (100K+ monthly searches) and low competition. Include keyword research in Spanish, but translate the keywords into English. Provide a list of 20 keywords for each micro niche, along with their search volume and competition score."
E-commerce Niche Research
"Identify 5 profitable niches for an e-commerce store. For each niche, provide examples of high-demand products, target audiences, and marketing strategies. Include keyword research and competitor analysis for each niche."
5. Advanced Content Creation Prompts
Podcast to Presentation Conversion
"Here's a YouTube video: [Insert Link]. Convert the content into a 10-slide presentation with sleek design and relevant images. Summarize the key points and include visuals to make the presentation engaging."
Interactive Content Creation
"Create an interactive quiz for my website on the topic '[Insert Topic]'. Include 10 questions with multiple-choice answers and explanations for each answer. Suggest how to integrate the quiz into my website and promote it on social media."
Content Repurposing Strategy
"Analyze my top-performing blog posts and suggest ways to repurpose them into other formats like videos, infographics, and podcasts. Provide a detailed plan for each format, including tools and platforms to use."
